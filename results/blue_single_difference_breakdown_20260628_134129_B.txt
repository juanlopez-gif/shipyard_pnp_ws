WHERE THE PREVIOUS 6.5 SECONDS CAME FROM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before the fix, the whole Bantam close/process/open block started late in
simulation because Bantam waited for Robot2 RETURN_HOME:

  Reality CLOSING_DOOR starts at:   77.009 s
  Old simulation DOOR_CLOSING:       83.500 s
  Delay:                              6.491 s

After the fix:

  Reality CLOSING_DOOR starts at:   77.009 s
  New simulation DOOR_CLOSING:       77.000 s
  Difference:                        -0.009 s


WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reality:
  robot2 MOVE_PIECE C2S2 -> BANTAM_BED runs from 48.339 s to 76.950 s.
  Bantam RUN_JOB starts at 76.988 s, immediately after robot2 completes the
  place-at-Bantam command.

Simulation:
  robot2 sequence is:
    PICK_C2S2       57.000 -> 60.500
    PLACE_BANTAM    60.500 -> 74.500
    CLEAR_BANTAM    74.500 -> 77.000

  Bantam waits only for CLEAR_BANTAM.
  Therefore Bantam door closing now starts at 77.000 s.

The model mismatch was:
  Simulation included a full Robot2 RETURN_HOME before Bantam could close.
  Reality starts Bantam RUN_JOB immediately after Robot2 completes the
  place-at-Bantam command.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

section. It was not the door duration itself; it was the gating before the door
was allowed to close.

The simulation now uses CLEAR_BANTAM instead of a full Robot2 RETURN_HOME before
Bantam starts, matching the real BLUE path much more closely.
